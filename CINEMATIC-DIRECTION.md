## Cinematic design bible
Feeling: the relief of a well-prepared desk. Narrative: individual pieces become a clear, ordered whole. Original world: an architectural navy room with silver drafting lines, warm paper and a single gold light source. No unrelated landscapes, stock-video montages or floating financial claims.

Materials: navy lacquer (#071522), translucent silver glass, warm ivory paper (#e9e9e3), brushed gold (#d9bc80). Key light from upper right, soft cool fill from left; low dark contact shadows ground objects. Gold is reserved for progress, identity and actions.

Camera: establish the glass document in the opening shot; a short desktop sticky scene gathers three translucent documents into one aligned folio as the visitor scrolls. Text remains stationary and readable. No scroll interception. Mobile uses a composed static folio and full readable content instead of a pinned camera.

Motion: ambient reflection 8–16s; interactive feedback 180–350ms; major scene transforms follow native scroll. Avoid adding fade-up to every item. Motion pauses when the scene is offscreen and respects reduced motion and the existing Pause control. Intro preserves original copy/logo with skip, Escape and replay. Ambient effects never carry essential information.

Type: Manrope for clear modern headings; Georgia italic for a small number of emotional statements; DM Sans for body; small uppercase chapter labels. Body remains at least 15px in narrative sections. Use space and hierarchy, not miniature text.

Interactions: service rows expose their complete description and destination without hover. Focus has the same affordances as pointer hover. Consultation fields retain native validation and clear retry states. HB contract unchanged. No custom cursor or sound.
